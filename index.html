Medical High-Value Care Simulator

This interactive module helps medical trainees practice high-value, context-dependent diagnostic decision-making. You can freely search and order tests, then receive feedback on value and see costs after submission.

What is High Value Care?

High value care is the delivery of the best possible health outcomes for patients at the lowest necessary cost. It does not entail ordering fewer tests, but rather, the most effective testing for the presentation while minimizing harms and waste.

Before you start

  • No multiple-choice spoilers: you choose your own diagnostic pathway.
  • Costs are hidden until you submit for each case.
  • Costs shown in this module are derived from the publicly available chargemaster for UCLA Ronald Reagan Medical Center, as of June 1, 2026. These are institutional list prices, not what any payer or patient actually pays.
  • High-value care is dynamic: what’s high value depends on the patient, timing, and pre-test probability.
  • This information is for educational purposes only and should not be considered medical advice or be used in place of clinician judgement.
